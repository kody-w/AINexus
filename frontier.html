🖐 Frontier out there mode

Your hands and your voice are the controller. Nothing is simulated — the same verbs an AI player uses.

point — move the cursor (your hand is the mouse)
rest on an orb — the ring fills in 2½s and snaps (hands-free)
rest on a person — open a ring of things to say; the same for a human or an AI
pinch — select it now, without waiting
open palm — walk forward; lean it to turn as you go
speak — “go to Crystal Caverns”, “turn left”, “ask, what is this place”, or just talk to the room

idle — press start hands (your camera never leaves this machine)

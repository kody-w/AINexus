Autodrive · control tower

Each tab is a person: the driver only ever sends real UI events — keydown to walk, mousemove to look, a click to enter a portal, the page's own chat input to speak. Every AI player is labelled as an AI to everyone in the room. Programs are declarative (a fixed verb set — never fetched JavaScript) and are verified against an append-only chain this repo owns: the tower recomputes every frame hash and refuses bytecode whose bytes do not match its sealed publication. Budgets stop a runaway; the journal is the receipt.
Origin note: injection works because the frames are same-origin. A cross-origin world cannot be driven, and this tower will say so rather than pretend.
